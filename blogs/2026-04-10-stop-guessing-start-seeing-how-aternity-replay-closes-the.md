Most Digital Employee Experience (DEX) platforms can tell IT teams when something breaks. Until now, none could show what actually happened. When employees call the service desk saying “it just didn’t work,” support teams are forced to guess, recreating issues after the fact, relying on incomplete user recollection, and combing through telemetry to piece together the story.
