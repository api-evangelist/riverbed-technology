For years, network operations teams had a relatively clear view of the environment they were responsible for. Users worked in offices, applications ran in data centers, and traffic traversed infrastructure IT owned and managed. Packets, flows, and infrastructure monitoring gave NetOps the visibility needed to understand performance and troubleshoot problems.
