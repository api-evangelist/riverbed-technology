Zero Trust has quickly become the gold standard for security. In fact, 81% of organizations have fully or partially implemented a Zero Trust model, while the remaining 19% are planning to (ZeroThreats Survey, August 2025​). ​ By enforcing continuous verification of every user, device, and app, organizations close the door on implicit trust and lateral movement.
