AI has moved beyond experimentation and into the IT operating model. Organizations are placing big bets on it to accelerate innovation, improve operational decision-making and transform how IT teams work. But as enterprises move toward agentic AI, many still question whether they’re ready to trust AI to take meaningful action without human oversight.
