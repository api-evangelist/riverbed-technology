# Riverbed Technology

> Riverbed is an enterprise unified observability and digital-experience-management (DEM) company. Its platform spans network, application, infrastructure, cloud, and end-user experience monitoring — including the Aternity DEM suite, AppResponse/NetProfiler/NetIM network observability, SteelHead WAN optimization / data acceleration, and the Riverbed IQ AIOps intelligence layer. Integrations are built and customized through Riverbed's secure APIs and pre-built connectors.

## Products
- [Aternity Digital Experience Management](https://www.riverbed.com/products/): Employee, mobile, and customer experience monitoring
- [Network Observability (AppResponse, NetProfiler, NetIM)](https://www.riverbed.com/products/): Packet, flow, and infrastructure monitoring
- [SteelHead / Data Express](https://www.riverbed.com/products/): WAN optimization and high-speed data movement
- [Riverbed IQ / AIOps](https://www.riverbed.com/products/): AI-driven detection and autonomous IT operations
